Square, 6px-radius icon-only control for toolbars, composers and table rows.

\`\`\`jsx
<IconButton name="search" label="Search" />
<IconButton name="more-horizontal" variant="outlined" size="sm" />
\`\`\`

Variants: ghost (default, warm ink wash on hover), outlined, solid (off-black). No scale-up hover — icon buttons only press down.
